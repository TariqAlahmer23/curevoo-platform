Interpretation
--------------
- CIViC increased external evidence coverage by 306 CIViC-only positive genes within the ranked-gene universe.
- High-confidence labels improved mean ROC-AUC relative to any-source labels (mean delta=+0.0748; F1 delta=+0.1080 across matching models). Cohort sizes differ, so this is a benchmark comparison, not a paired clinical test.
- The best raw-feature model achieved ROC-AUC=0.7600 and F1=0.5962 for high_confidence; this is moderate reproduction of external labels and does not establish clinical validity.
