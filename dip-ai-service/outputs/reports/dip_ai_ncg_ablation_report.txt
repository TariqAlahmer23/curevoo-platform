DIP-AI NCG Scientific Ablation Study
====================================

Status: COMPLETED
Evaluation: stratified_5_fold_cross_validation
Best-model rule: highest out-of-fold ROC-AUC within each experiment; F1 is the tie-breaker.

Interpretation
--------------
- Effect of clean negatives: with raw features, mean ΔROC-AUC=-0.0012, ΔF1=+0.0046, ΔMCC=+0.0111; with raw plus normal lung TPM, mean ΔROC-AUC=+0.0044, ΔF1=+0.0059, ΔMCC=+0.0127. A gain here reflects the negative-selection rule and may mean the clean negatives are easier to separate.
- Effect of raw safety feature normal_lung_tpm: with random negatives, mean ΔROC-AUC=+0.0016, ΔF1=-0.0026, ΔMCC=+0.0018; with clean negatives, mean ΔROC-AUC=+0.0072, ΔF1=-0.0013, ΔMCC=+0.0034.
- Effect of derived ranking/safety scores on the fixed clean dataset: mean ΔROC-AUC=+0.0014, ΔF1=+0.0109, ΔMCC=+0.0082.
- Effect of curated knowledge features on the same fixed clean dataset: mean ΔROC-AUC=+0.0003, ΔF1=+0.0010, ΔMCC=+0.0017.

Scientific Warning
------------------
No dominant post-derived_scores or post-knowledge_augmented ROC-AUC increase was detected under the predefined >0.02 comparison rule. This still does not establish clinical validity.
